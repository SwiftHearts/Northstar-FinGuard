# Risk Disclosure Handbook

**Document ID:** NWP-RDH-02
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.4
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer

## 1. Purpose

This handbook defines the mandatory risk disclosure language that must accompany any client-facing material, advisory agreement, or communication that discusses investment products, strategies, or expected outcomes offered by Northstar Wealth Partners. It exists to ensure clients understand the risks of loss inherent in investing before relying on Firm advice.

## 2. General Investment Risk Disclosure

The following baseline disclosure must appear on all materials that reference investment performance, portfolio strategy, or account growth, in a font size no smaller than the surrounding body text:

> "All investments involve risk, including the possible loss of principal. Past performance is not indicative of future results. There is no guarantee that any investment strategy will achieve its objectives."

This disclosure may not be shortened, paraphrased into a weaker form, or placed in a location that is not reasonably visible to the reader (e.g., buried in a footer smaller than 8pt or hidden behind a "read more" interaction without a clear prompt).

## 3. Risk Categories Requiring Specific Disclosure

| Risk Type | When Disclosure Is Required | Required Language Element |
|---|---|---|
| Market Risk | Any equity or multi-asset strategy | Values fluctuate with market conditions and may decline |
| Concentration Risk | Portfolios with >20% in a single sector or issuer | Lack of diversification may increase volatility |
| Liquidity Risk | Alternative investments, non-traded REITs, private funds | Investment may not be readily convertible to cash |
| Interest Rate Risk | Fixed income strategies | Bond values move inversely to interest rate changes |
| Currency Risk | International or global strategies | Currency fluctuations may affect returns |
| Leverage Risk | Any strategy using margin or leveraged instruments | Leverage can magnify both gains and losses |

## 4. Performance Advertising Disclosure Requirements

Any presentation of historical performance (composite, model, or account-level) must include, at minimum:

1. Whether results are gross or net of fees (net-of-fees preferred and required for retail materials)
2. The time period covered and whether it represents a full market cycle
3. A statement that past performance does not guarantee future results
4. The benchmark used for comparison, if any, and why it is an appropriate comparison
5. Whether the results are hypothetical, back-tested, or actual client results

## 5. Prohibited Risk Minimization Language

The following categories of language are prohibited in any client communication or marketing material, as they misrepresent the risk of loss:

- "Guaranteed return" or "guaranteed growth" applied to any market-based investment
- "No risk" or "risk-free" applied to anything other than FDIC-insured cash deposits within insured limits
- "Safe as a savings account" applied to securities-based investments
- Implying that diversification eliminates the possibility of loss
- Describing volatility as a purely short-term phenomenon without acknowledging potential for sustained loss

## 6. Client Acknowledgment

New clients must sign the Risk Disclosure Acknowledgment (Form NWP-RDH-ACK) prior to account funding, confirming they have received and reviewed this handbook's disclosures relevant to their selected strategy. IARs must retain a copy in the client file per the recordkeeping requirements in the Client Communications Policy (NWP-CCP-06).

## 7. Annual Review

The Chief Compliance Officer reviews this handbook annually, and immediately following any material change in applicable SEC or FINRA risk disclosure requirements.
