# Marketing Compliance Guidelines

**Document ID:** NWP-MKT-03
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 4.1
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Regulatory Basis:** SEC Marketing Rule (17 CFR 275.206(4)-1), FINRA Rule 2210

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document governs all "advertisements" as defined under the SEC Marketing Rule — any communication to more than one retail investor, or any communication to a single retail investor if it is disseminated broadly (email blasts, social media, website content, brochures, pitch decks, webinars). It applies to Firm-produced content and content produced by IARs, including personal social media used in a professional capacity.

## 2. General Prohibitions

No advertisement may:

1. Make an untrue statement of material fact, or omit a fact necessary to make the statement not misleading
2. Make a material claim that is unsubstantiated
3. Discuss any potential benefit without providing fair and balanced treatment of associated risks or limitations
4. Include or exclude performance results in a manner that is not fair and balanced
5. Discuss or imply that Northstar's services or strategies have been approved or endorsed by any regulatory body

## 3. Testimonials and Endorsements

Client testimonials and third-party endorsements are permitted only when all of the following conditions are met:

- A clear and prominent disclosure is included stating whether the person is a client and whether compensation was provided
- Material conflicts of interest related to the testimonial or endorsement are disclosed
- A written agreement is in place for any compensated endorser, retained in the compliance file
- The testimonial does not state or imply guaranteed future performance

## 4. Performance Advertising Rules

Performance advertising must follow the Risk Disclosure Handbook (NWP-RDH-02) Section 4 requirements in full. In addition:

- **Cherry-picking is prohibited.** A firm may not selectively present only its best-performing accounts, time periods, or composites while excluding comparable results that would give a less favorable impression.
- **Extracted performance** (a subset of a portfolio, e.g., "our technology holdings returned 40%") must be accompanied by, or offer promptly upon request, the performance of the total portfolio from which it was extracted.
- **Hypothetical performance** (model portfolios, backtests, targeted/projected returns) may only be shown to audiences for whom the Firm has adopted policies reasonably designed to ensure relevance, and must include disclosure of the criteria and methodology used, and the risks and limitations of hypothetical performance.
- Predecessor performance from an IAR's prior firm may only be used if it is not misleading and all relevant disclosures accompany it.

## 5. Required Standard Disclosures on Marketing Material

Every piece of marketing material must include:

1. The general investment risk disclosure from NWP-RDH-02 Section 2
2. Northstar's Form CRS or a reference to where it can be obtained, where applicable
3. A statement that Northstar is an SEC-registered investment adviser, and that registration does not imply a certain level of skill or training
4. Firm contact information and, for digital content, a compliant unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism where applicable

## 6. Prohibited Language List

The following categories of phrases and their close variants are banned in any advertisement without further compliance review and CCO sign-off, because they have a high likelihood of being misleading. The specific prohibited categories are set out in Sections 6.1 through 6.5 below.

## 6.1 Guarantees and Risk-Free Language

"Guaranteed," "risk-free," "can't lose," "no downside," and closely related phrasing implying that an investment carries no possibility of loss are prohibited in any advertisement. Investing inherently involves risk of loss, including of principal, and no advertisement may state or imply otherwise regardless of the underlying strategy's actual risk profile.

## 6.2 Absolute Performance Claims

"Beat the market every year" and similar absolute or unqualified performance claims are prohibited. Any performance claim must be presented consistent with the Performance Advertising Rules in Section 4 above and the Risk Disclosure Handbook (NWP-RDH-02) Section 4, including all required time-period, benchmark, and fee context.

## 6.3 Unsubstantiated Comparative Claims

"Better than [competitor]" and similar comparative claims are prohibited unless supported by a documented, substantiated, apples-to-apples comparison, disclosed alongside the claim, covering the same time period, fee structure, and risk profile for both parties being compared.

## 6.4 Misleading Fee Claims

The word "free," or any claim that a service has no cost, is prohibited for any service that has an associated fee elsewhere in the client relationship, unless that fee is clearly and conspicuously disclosed immediately adjacent to the claim. This applies even when the fee is waived only temporarily or only for a subset of clients.

## 6.5 Unqualified Superlatives

Unqualified superlatives such as "best," "top," or "#1" are prohibited unless accompanied by a citation to the specific, current, and methodologically sound ranking source, including the name of the ranking organization, the criteria used, and the date of the ranking. A ranking claim without this citation is considered an unsubstantiated superlative claim regardless of whether the underlying ranking is genuine.

## 7. Review and Approval Workflow

All advertisements must be submitted to compliance review prior to publication using the Marketing Review Request (Form NWP-MKT-REV). Reviews are triaged by risk tier per the Regulatory Review Playbook (NWP-RRP-07). No material may be published, posted, or distributed without a logged compliance approval, except pre-approved evergreen templates refreshed no more than 90 days prior.

## 8. Social Media Policy

IARs using social media in a professional capacity must use only Firm-approved, archived platforms. Personal opinions about specific securities ("hot takes") are prohibited. All posts are subject to the same substantiation and disclosure requirements as any other advertisement and are subject to retrospective compliance review and archiving under NWP-CCP-06.

## 9. Recordkeeping

Copies of all advertisements, the names of persons who approved them, and the substantiation for any performance or factual claim must be retained for five years, the first two in an easily accessible place, per SEC Rule 204-2.
