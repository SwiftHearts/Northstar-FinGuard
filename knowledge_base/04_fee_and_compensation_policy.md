# Fee and Compensation Policy

**Document ID:** NWP-FCP-04
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.1
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer / Chief Financial Officer

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how Northstar Wealth Partners charges, discloses, and communicates fees and compensation, and how the Firm manages conflicts of interest that arise from its compensation structure. Any marketing or client communication that references cost, pricing, or "free" services must be consistent with this policy.

## 2. Standard Fee Schedule

Northstar charges an asset-based advisory fee, billed quarterly in advance, based on assets under management (AUM):

| AUM Tier | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| First $1,000,000 | 1.00% |
| Next $2,000,000 ($1M–$3M) | 0.80% |
| Next $2,000,000 ($3M–$5M) | 0.60% |
| Above $5,000,000 | 0.40% |

Fees are tiered (blended across bands), not a flat rate applied to the entire balance once a threshold is crossed. The fee schedule is negotiable in writing for institutional and certain legacy accounts, and any negotiated rate must be documented in the client's Investment Advisory Agreement.

## 3. What Is and Is Not Included

The advisory fee covers ongoing portfolio management, quarterly performance reporting, and standard financial planning consultations. The advisory fee does NOT cover:

- Underlying fund expense ratios (paid indirectly by the client to the fund company)
- Custodial fees charged by the qualified custodian
- Transaction costs or commissions on certain non-advisory brokerage transactions
- Third-party manager fees for sub-advised or alternative investment sleeves

Any marketing claim describing Northstar's services as having "one simple fee" or similar must disclose, adjacent to the claim, that underlying fund and custodial costs are separate and borne by the client.

## 4. Prohibition on Misleading Fee Claims

No communication may describe Northstar's fee structure as "free," "no-cost," or "commission-free" unless every component of the client relationship referenced is genuinely free of charge to the client, with no offsetting compensation received by the Firm or its IARs from third parties in connection with that service. Where a service is offered at no direct charge but the Firm receives indirect compensation (e.g., revenue sharing, referral fees), that arrangement must be disclosed prominently, not in a footnote alone.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest Arising from Compensation

Northstar identifies the following as material conflicts requiring disclosure in Form ADV Part 2A and, where applicable, in client-facing summaries:

- IAR compensation that varies based on the investment product or share class recommended
- Revenue sharing arrangements with custodians or fund sponsors
- Referral fees paid or received for client introductions
- Compensation tied to AUM growth, which creates an incentive to recommend increasing assets under management rather than, for example, debt paydown or outside investment

IARs may not receive compensation from a third party in connection with advice given to a client without prior written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer and disclosure to the affected client.

## 6. Fee Comparison Claims

Any material comparing Northstar's fees to a competitor's, an industry average, or a robo-advisor must cite the specific, current, and methodologically documented source of the comparison figure. Comparisons must account for differences in included services (e.g., comparing an AUM fee against a flat planning fee without normalizing for scope is prohibited as misleading).

## 7. Disclosure Timing

Fee schedules and conflict disclosures must be provided to prospective clients before or at the time of entering into an advisory agreement, and the current Form ADV Part 2A must be offered annually to existing clients without request, per SEC requirements.

## 8. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the CCO and CFO and updated immediately upon any fee schedule change, which also triggers a Form ADV amendment per the Regulatory Review Playbook (NWP-RRP-07).
