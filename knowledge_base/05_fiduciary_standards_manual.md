# Fiduciary Standards Manual

**Document ID:** NWP-FSM-05
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Regulatory Basis:** Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (Sections 206(1)-(2)), fiduciary duty of care and loyalty

## 1. Purpose

Northstar Wealth Partners is a registered investment adviser and, as such, owes each client a fiduciary duty under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. This manual translates that legal duty into operational standards for every IAR, portfolio manager, and employee who interacts with client accounts or communications.

## 2. Duty of Care

The duty of care requires Northstar to act in the client's best interest based on the client's objectives, taking reasonable steps to:

- Obtain and analyze sufficient information about the client's financial situation, risk tolerance, and investment objectives before providing advice
- Provide advice and monitoring over the course of the relationship consistent with the scope of the engagement
- Seek best execution of client transactions, evaluating the full range and quality of a broker-dealer's execution services, not price alone
- Provide advice that is suitable and in the client's best interest at the time it is given, and periodically thereafter within the agreed scope of the relationship

## 3. Duty of Loyalty

The duty of loyalty requires Northstar to place client interests ahead of the Firm's or its personnel's own interests. This means:

- Eliminating or making full and fair disclosure of all material conflicts of interest that could affect the impartiality of advice
- Never placing Firm or IAR compensation considerations ahead of client best interest in a specific recommendation
- Obtaining informed consent from clients where a conflict cannot be fully eliminated

## 4. Distinguishing Fiduciary Standard from Suitability/Reg BI

Northstar operates exclusively under the Investment Advisers Act fiduciary standard, which is a higher bar than the SEC's Regulation Best Interest ("Reg BI") standard applicable to broker-dealers. Marketing or client communications must not blur this distinction. It is prohibited to:

- Claim broker-dealer competitors are "not required to act in your best interest" without appropriate nuance about Reg BI (this is a defensible factual point but must be presented accurately, not as blanket disparagement)
- Imply that "fiduciary" is a marketing label rather than a specific, enforceable legal standard the Firm can substantiate

## 5. Conflict of Interest Identification and Mitigation

All identified conflicts (see also Fee and Compensation Policy, NWP-FCP-04, Section 5) are logged in the Firm's Conflicts Inventory, reviewed quarterly by the CCO, and categorized as:

- **Eliminated** — the underlying arrangement was terminated
- **Mitigated** — structural changes reduce the conflict's influence (e.g., a compensation committee independent of individual recommendations)
- **Disclosed** — the conflict remains but is fully and clearly disclosed to affected clients

## 6. Best Execution Review

The Best Execution Committee reviews execution quality across custodians and broker-dealers at least quarterly, documenting price, speed, likelihood of execution, and overall service quality. Any material finding that execution quality has degraded must be escalated to the CCO within five business days.

## 7. Documentation Standard

Every recommendation involving a change in strategy, product, or significant reallocation must be documented with the rationale tying the recommendation to the client's stated objectives and risk tolerance, retained in the client file. Undocumented material recommendations are treated as a compliance exception under the Regulatory Review Playbook (NWP-RRP-07).

## 8. Training and Attestation

All IARs must complete annual fiduciary duty training and sign an attestation confirming understanding of the duties of care and loyalty described in this manual. New hires complete this training within 30 days of their start date.
