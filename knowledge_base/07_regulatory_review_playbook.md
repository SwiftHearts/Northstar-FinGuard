# Regulatory Review Playbook

**Document ID:** NWP-RRP-07
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 1.6
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer

## 1. Purpose

This playbook defines how Northstar Wealth Partners triages, reviews, escalates, and remediates compliance risks identified in marketing materials, client communications, and general business practices. It is the operational backbone referenced by every other policy in this knowledge base when a violation, exception, or ambiguous case is found.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) | Final authority on compliance determinations; owns escalations at Tier 3 and above |
| Compliance Reviewer | First-line review of marketing submissions and flagged communications |
| Marketing Review Committee | Cross-functional sign-off for Tier 2+ marketing material |
| IAR / Content Author | Submits material for review; responsible for substantiation of factual claims |
| Best Execution Committee | Reviews execution-quality escalations per NWP-FSM-05 Section 6 |

## 3. Risk Severity Tiers

Every finding — whether from a marketing review, a supervisory spot-check, or an automated compliance check — is classified into one of four severity tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Low:** Stylistic or minor disclosure formatting issues (e.g., disclosure present but font size borderline). Remediate before publication; no escalation required.
- **Tier 2 — Moderate:** A claim lacks full substantiation, or a required disclosure is missing but the underlying claim is not overtly misleading. Requires Compliance Reviewer sign-off after correction; logged in the Compliance Exception Register.
- **Tier 3 — High:** A claim uses prohibited language (per NWP-MKT-03 Section 6) or misrepresents risk, fees, or performance in a way likely to mislead a reasonable investor. Requires CCO review, mandatory revision, and root-cause documentation.
- **Tier 4 — Critical:** A published (already distributed) communication contains a materially false or misleading statement, an unauthorized guarantee, or a fiduciary duty breach. Requires immediate takedown/correction, CCO and senior management notification within 24 hours, and evaluation of whether the matter is reportable to the SEC or FINRA.

## 4. Escalation Workflow

1. **Detection** — issue identified via pre-publication review, supervisory sampling, automated screening, or client complaint (see NWP-CCP-06 Section 6).
2. **Triage** — Compliance Reviewer assigns a severity tier within one business day of detection.
3. **Routing** — Tier 1–2 findings are returned to the content author for correction. Tier 3–4 findings are routed directly to the CCO and, for Tier 4, to senior management concurrently.
4. **Remediation** — corrected material is resubmitted for review; original and corrected versions are both retained per NWP-MKT-03 Section 9 and NWP-CCP-06 Section 7.
5. **Root Cause and Closure** — Tier 3 and Tier 4 findings require a documented root cause (e.g., training gap, process gap, individual error) and, where applicable, a corrective action plan with an owner and due date.

## 5. Remediation Service-Level Targets

| Tier | Acknowledgment | Remediation Target |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Same business day | Before publication |
| Tier 2 | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| Tier 3 | Same business day | 5 business days |
| Tier 4 | Immediate (within hours) | 24 hours for takedown; full remediation plan within 5 business days |

## 6. Regulatory Filing Triggers

The CCO evaluates whether the following require a regulatory filing or amendment:

- Any Tier 4 finding involving client-facing fee or performance misrepresentation → potential Form ADV amendment and self-assessment of Rule 206(4)-1 compliance
- Any change to the standard fee schedule (NWP-FCP-04) → Form ADV Part 2A amendment within required timeframe
- Any material change to conflicts of interest disclosures (NWP-FSM-05 Section 5) → Form ADV update and client notification evaluation

## 7. Audit Trail Requirements

Every review decision, regardless of tier, must be logged with: reviewer identity, timestamp, severity classification, rationale, and final disposition. This audit trail is retained for no less than five years per SEC Rule 204-2 and must be produced in full during any regulatory examination or internal audit.

## 8. Periodic Program Review

The CCO conducts an annual review of the overall compliance program's effectiveness, including a review of Compliance Exception Register trends by tier and by policy area, to identify systemic gaps requiring policy updates across this knowledge base.
