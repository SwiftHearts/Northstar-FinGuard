# Client Communications Policy

**Document ID:** NWP-CCP-06
**Firm:** Northstar Wealth Partners
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Version:** 3.0
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Regulatory Basis:** SEC Rule 204-2 (Books and Records), FINRA Rule 2210

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs all communications between Northstar personnel and clients or prospective clients, including email, text/SMS, social media, client portal messaging, webinars, and printed correspondence. It works together with the Marketing Compliance Guidelines (NWP-MKT-03), which governs advertisements specifically, and this policy, which governs individualized and ongoing client communication.

## 2. Approved Communication Channels

Clients may only be communicated with regarding Firm business through channels the Firm can archive and supervise:

- Firm email (personal email addresses may not be used for client business)
- The Firm-issued client portal
- Firm-approved text messaging platform (archived automatically)
- Firm-approved social media accounts (see NWP-MKT-03 Section 8)

Use of unapproved channels (personal WhatsApp, personal social media DMs, unarchived text messages) for substantive client business is a compliance violation subject to escalation under the Regulatory Review Playbook (NWP-RRP-07).

## 3. Required Elements in Individualized Communications

Any communication that discusses a specific client's account, performance, or a specific recommendation must:

- Be consistent with the client's documented risk tolerance and investment objectives on file
- Include the general risk disclosure (NWP-RDH-02 Section 2) when discussing performance or projected outcomes
- Avoid promissory language about future account value or returns

## 4. Prohibited Communication Practices

- Providing personalized investment advice through an unsupervised or unarchived channel
- Making forward-looking statements about specific account performance ("your account will reach $X by Y date")
- Sharing another client's information, even anonymized, in a way that could reasonably identify them, without consent
- Responding to client complaints informally without logging them per Section 6

## 5. Communication Review Tiers

Individualized client communications are generally not pre-reviewed but are subject to supervisory spot-checks. Communications that are broadcast to more than one recipient, or that discuss performance, new products, or market commentary distributed broadly, are treated as advertisements and must follow the NWP-MKT-03 review workflow instead of this section.

## 6. Complaint Handling

Any expression of dissatisfaction, written or verbal, that alleges a specific grievance regarding advice, execution, fees, or a recommendation must be logged in the Complaint Register within one business day, regardless of the channel it arrived on or whether the client explicitly calls it a "complaint." Complaints are escalated per the Regulatory Review Playbook (NWP-RRP-07) Section 4.

## 7. Recordkeeping Requirements

Per SEC Rule 204-2, Northstar retains:

- All communications relating to recommendations or advice given, for a minimum of five years (first two years in an easily accessible location)
- Complaint records for the life of the client relationship plus five years
- Archived social media and text communications, captured at the time of posting/sending, not reconstructed after the fact

Employees may not delete, alter, or use auto-deleting messaging features for any Firm-business communication.

## 8. Supervisory Review

Designated supervisors conduct periodic (no less than quarterly) sampling of archived individualized communications to check for consistency with this policy and with client suitability documentation. Findings are reported to the CCO.
